IPsec on Windows now sends your traffic into the VPN, and reconnects without hanging.

FortiConnect 0.5.0 updates itself to this version (Settings → Updates).

## Downloads

- Windows: `FortiConnect-Setup-0.5.1.exe` (includes OpenConnect)
- macOS (Apple silicon): `FortiConnect-0.5.1-mac-arm64.dmg`
- Linux: `FortiConnect-0.5.1-x86_64.AppImage`

## Fixes

- **IPsec connected but nothing behind the VPN could be reached.** Windows kept sending traffic out of the normal
  network: the VPN adapter did not get its address, and "all traffic" did not win over the normal default route. The
  VPN helper now sets up its own IPsec adapter ("FortiConnect IPsec") itself: address, DNS, a route to the FortiGate
  outside the VPN, and the VPN's routes, which always win now. Disconnect removes exactly what it added.
- **Reconnecting hung at "Setting up the VPN adapter".** The IPsec adapter is now kept between connections instead of
  being removed and created again each time.
- When the FortiGate names its company networks (split tunnel), only those go through the VPN, as with FortiClient.
- About 15 seconds after connecting, the Log page says how many packets went to the FortiGate and how many came back.

The VPN helper is updated to version 5 the first time you connect (Windows asks for administrator rights once).

## Unsigned builds

On Windows, SmartScreen may warn on first run: choose **More info → Run anyway**. On macOS, right-click the app and
choose **Open** the first time; macOS copies announce updates instead of installing them.
