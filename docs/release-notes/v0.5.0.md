IPsec IKEv2 on Windows now carries traffic: FortiGate dial-up tunnels made for FortiClient work in FortiConnect.

FortiConnect 0.4.x updates itself to this version (Settings → Updates).

## Downloads

- Windows: `FortiConnect-Setup-0.5.0.exe` (includes OpenConnect)
- macOS (Apple silicon): `FortiConnect-0.5.0-mac-arm64.dmg`
- Linux: `FortiConnect-0.5.0-x86_64.AppImage`

## New

- **IPsec IKEv2 tunnels carry your traffic (step 2 of 3).** An IPsec profile with **IKEv2 + EAP** now connects for
  real: FortiConnect logs in, the VPN helper sets up the FortiConnect network adapter with the address, DNS and routes
  the FortiGate hands out, and your traffic travels encrypted to the FortiGate. Disconnect puts your network settings
  back. FortiConnect also answers the FortiGate's "are you still there?" checks and notices when the tunnel is ended.
- The VPN helper is updated to version 4 the first time you connect with IPsec (Windows asks for administrator rights
  once).

## Known limits

- This is the first version that carries IPsec traffic: if pages stop loading while connected, press Disconnect,
  which restores your network, and send the Log page to the developer.
- New keys during a long connection are not supported yet: a tunnel may drop after about 12 hours and reconnect.

## Unsigned builds

On Windows, SmartScreen may warn on first run: choose **More info → Run anyway**. On macOS, right-click the app and
choose **Open** the first time; macOS copies announce updates instead of installing them.
